      The 2 a.m. Call: scene 1

      Personal portfolio demo. Fictional patient. Clinical content requires review by a nursing subject matter expert.

      You are the night nurse. Walk around a patient's room in first person and examine what is at the bedside. What you observe is saved in your notes. Then you call the provider. This is the first scene of an immersive version of the simulation (beta).

      Controls

      • Look: drag with a mouse or finger, or use the Turn and Look buttons.
      • Move: the Go to buttons, or arrow keys or W A S D while the 3D view has focus.
      • Turn with the keyboard: Q and E, or Shift with the arrow keys.
      • Examine: select an object in the room or in the Objects nearby list.
      • Leave the 3D view: press Tab. Nothing traps the keyboard.
      Movement style

      Smooth walking is switched to short steps while Reduce motion is on.

      Help and controls

      • Move: arrow keys or W A S D while the 3D view has focus. In jump style each press is one step; in smooth style hold the key.
      • Turn: Q and E, or Shift with the left and right arrows. Shift with up and down looks up and down.
      • Look: drag with a mouse or finger, or use the Turn left, Turn right, Look up and Look down buttons.
      • Go to: the buttons at the bottom left take you to a spot in the room.
      • Examine: select a marker in the room, click the object, or choose it in Objects nearby. Findings appear in a card and are added to Notes.
      • Finish: choose "I'm ready to call the provider". You can finish at any time.
      • Leave the 3D view: press Tab. Press Escape to close this window.
      • Escape closes the topmost panel or window you can close, and returns focus to the control that opened it.
